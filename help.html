Help & Recovery

If something's not working — the answer is usually here.

Flipped is a tool, not a trap. Every state your iPhone can get into during setup is recoverable. Below are the most common situations, each with the exact steps to get back to a working phone. Pick the section that matches what you're seeing.

Can't find your situation here? Email support@useflipped.com with a screenshot of where you're stuck. Pete (the founder) answers personally — usually within a few hours.

My iPhone is stuck on the "Device Management" screen

You're on the iOS screen that says something like "Device Management — This iPhone is owned by Flipped" and you don't know what to tap. You haven't enrolled yet — you can back out safely.

Do this:

  1. On your iPhone, tap Back (top-left) until you exit the Device Management screen.
  2. Open the Mac app. It should detect that setup didn't finish and show "Restart Setup" — tap it.
  3. If the Mac app doesn't detect anything, quit Flipped (Cmd+Q) and reopen it. Reopening triggers a fresh check of your iPhone's state.

Why this works: until you actually tap "Enroll This iPhone," your iPhone hasn't accepted Flipped's management. Backing out just leaves you where you were before setup.

Setup says "Enrollment Failed" or hangs on "Enroll This iPhone"

You tapped "Enroll This iPhone" and either got an error, or the screen just won't move. This is rare but recoverable.

Try these in order:

  1. Wait 30 seconds. Sometimes iOS takes a moment to register the enrollment, especially on slow cellular or congested Wi-Fi.
  2. Force-quit Flipped on your Mac (Cmd+Q, then reopen). The app will re-check your iPhone's state and offer "Restart Setup" if needed.
  3. From the Mac app, open Settings → Remove from iPhone. This cleanly strips any half-installed profile from your iPhone so you can restart from a clean state.
  4. Restart your iPhone (hold side button + volume until the slider appears). Sometimes iOS just needs a reboot to clear a stuck enrollment.
  5. Still stuck? Email support@useflipped.com — include your email + which step you tapped last. Pete can run a one-curl reset on his end that clears your iPhone's stuck state remotely.

The Mac app says "Waiting for iPhone…" and never moves

The Mac app is mid-setup but the iPhone hasn't responded for a while. This usually means iOS is confused about the trust state or the USB connection dropped.

Do this:

  1. Unlock your iPhone. Setup tasks pause if the iPhone is locked.
  2. Check for a "Trust This Computer?" prompt on your iPhone. If you see it, tap Trust + enter your passcode.
  3. Unplug and replug the USB cable — use a different port if you have one. Bad cables cause this more often than anything else.
  4. From the Mac app, tap "Reset Trust" (Settings → Get Help → Reset Trust). This clears the Mac's stored pairing and lets iOS prompt for trust fresh.
  5. Still hanging after 5 minutes? Same as above — email support and we'll unstick you remotely.

My Mac doesn't see my iPhone over USB

This is almost always a cable or trust-prompt issue.

  1. Try a different USB cable. Many cables are charge-only and don't carry data. The original Apple Lightning/USB-C cable always works.
  2. Try a different USB port on your Mac. Some hubs and USB-C dongles cause issues.
  3. Unlock your iPhone and look for a "Trust This Computer?" dialog — your Mac is invisible to Flipped until you tap Trust.
  4. Open Finder on your Mac. Does your iPhone appear in the sidebar? If no, the cable or trust state is the issue. If yes, Flipped should also see it — quit and reopen Flipped.

I just want to remove Flipped from my iPhone

The clean way (takes 30 seconds):

  1. Plug your iPhone into your Mac via USB.
  2. Open Flipped → Settings → tap "Remove from iPhone."
  3. Wait ~10 seconds. Your iPhone will reboot. Done.

That's it. No factory reset, no data loss, no complications. Flipped + the supervision profile are stripped cleanly; your iPhone returns to exactly how it was before you installed Flipped.

I deleted Flipped from my Mac but my iPhone still has it

Common mistake — uninstalling the Mac app DOESN'T remove the management profile from your iPhone. The iPhone still thinks it's being managed by Flipped, but there's no Mac app to remove that management cleanly.

Fix:

  1. Reinstall Flipped on your Mac (useflipped.com — download the latest DMG).
  2. Sign in with the email you used before.
  3. Plug your iPhone in.
  4. Open Flipped → Settings → "Remove from iPhone."
  5. Once your iPhone has been cleaned up, you can uninstall Flipped from the Mac for good.

If you can't reinstall Flipped on the Mac for any reason, the factory-reset path below works as a last resort.

Last resort — factory reset

You almost certainly don't need this. Every option above is preferable. But if you're truly stuck — no Mac access, can't get to support, need your iPhone right now — factory reset always works.

Important: factory reset wipes your iPhone. Make sure you have a recent iCloud or Mac backup before doing this so you can restore.

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Erase All Content and Settings.
  2. Confirm + wait. Your iPhone reboots into the welcome screen.
  3. Set up the iPhone fresh, or restore from your most recent backup.

After the reset, Flipped is fully gone. If you want to use Flipped again later, just walk through setup from your Mac normally.
