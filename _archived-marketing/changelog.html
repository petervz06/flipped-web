Changelog

Every release ships through here. Newest first.

1.0.92
July 29, 2026
Mac app
  • If your Mac doesn't see your iPhone, Flipped waits a full minute before saying so — it used to worry after eight seconds, while you were still plugging in. And it leads with the fix that usually works: unplug, plug back in, tap Trust.
  • A simpler opening screen. What you need for setup now appears on the setup steps themselves.
1.0.91
July 29, 2026
Mac app
  • Signing in no longer makes you repeat setup. If your iPhone is already connected, you go straight into the app.
  • Signing back in now works the way signing up does — enter your email, get a code. The old screen asked for a password or a license key, which most accounts never had.
  • Already have an account? The welcome screen says so now, and takes you straight to sign-in — no sitting through setup a second time.
  • Flipped is far less likely to ask you to sign in at all. Coming back after a few days no longer risks ending your session by mistake.

Mac app
  • Support can now fine-tune profiles per account — including which apps stay locked into every profile. If we haven't adjusted your account with you, nothing changes.
1.0.87
July 28, 2026
Mac app
  • Every schedule now picks its own profile. Strict mornings, lighter afternoons — each schedule carries the profile you chose for it, and editing a profile updates every schedule that uses it. Your phone shows each schedule's profile too.
  • Text across the whole app is brighter and easier to read.
  • When setup or a flip hits a wall, the app reports exactly what happened so support can fix your case fast — usually before you even write in.
1.0.86
July 15, 2026
Mac app
  • Check-ins pause the clock: your session timer freezes during a 15-minute check-in and the time is added back — a 4-hour focus is 4 hours of blocked time. The app says "CHECK-IN — APPS ARE BACK" instead of pretending it's retrying.
  • If a flip can't go through, the app now tells you exactly why and what to do — including when you're signed in with a different email than you paid with.
  • Settings shows your subscription — plan, renewal date, and one-click billing management.
  • Sign-in reminds you to use your checkout email, and setup says up front it takes about 5 minutes.

1.0.10
May 3, 2026
Settings → Get Help
  • "Copy Diagnostics" button puts a structured snapshot (version, device, active session, last error) on the clipboard. No password / token in the blob.
  • "Send Feedback" textarea — backend logs at WARNING (Sentry-captured) and writes a user.feedback event so the admin timeline shows full context.
Settings → Danger
  • "Export My Data" downloads a JSON of every row tied to your account (GDPR Article 20).
