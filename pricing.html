PRICING FAQ

Questions you might have.

What happens right after I buy?

We email you the download link instantly. On your Mac: open the app, sign in with your checkout email (6-digit code, no password), plug in your iPhone once with a cable. About 5 minutes total — after that, flipping is one tap and works wirelessly.

Do I need a Mac?

Yes — and that's the whole trick. Your Mac is the key: it's the only thing that flips and unflips your iPhone, which is why Flipped has no in-the-moment escape hatch. Any Mac on macOS 14 or newer works. Buying on your phone right now? Totally fine — we email you the download link, and setup waits until you're at your Mac.

When am I charged?

Once, at checkout. $99, and that's the last you'll hear from us about money — no renewal, no expiry, nothing to remember to cancel.

What if it's not for me?

Email support@useflipped.com within 30 days and we'll refund you in full. No questions, no exit survey, no talking you out of it.

Is one payment really it?

Yes. One $99 payment covers every feature and every future update to Flipped. If the company shuts down (which we don't intend), you keep access for as long as Apple's iOS supervision channel keeps working — the moat is in the OS, not on our servers.

I'm already on a monthly or annual plan — what happens to me?

Nothing changes. Your plan keeps working exactly as it does today. If you'd rather switch to the one-time price, email support@useflipped.com and we'll sort it out.

Family plan? Team licensing?

Not on the self-serve checkout yet — email support@useflipped.com. Team licenses are negotiated case by case.
