PRICING FAQ

Questions you might have.

What happens right after I buy?

We email you the download link instantly. On your Mac: open the app, sign in with your checkout email (6-digit code, no password), plug in your iPhone once with a cable. About 5 minutes total — after that, flipping is one tap and works wirelessly.

Do I need a Mac?

Yes — and that's the whole trick. Your Mac is the key: it's the only thing that flips and unflips your iPhone, which is why Flipped has no in-the-moment escape hatch. Any Mac on macOS 14 or newer works. Buying on your phone right now? Totally fine — we email you the download link, and setup waits until you're at your Mac.

When am I charged?

After your 3-day free trial. Monthly and Annual start free — your card is charged on day 3 unless you cancel first (one click, no questions asked). They renew automatically after that. Lifetime is a single payment.

What's the cancellation policy?

Monthly: cancel with one click in the customer portal — applies at the end of your billing period.

Annual: prorated refund within 14 days of renewal — cancel and recover most of your money.

Lifetime: 30-day no-questions-asked refund. Email support@useflipped.com.

Why is the Annual plan cheaper per month?

Paying yearly removes the churn-rate guesswork and lowers our payment-processing overhead. That saving — about two months free vs paying monthly — passes back to you. We'd rather have you committed than re-selling you every month.

Is Lifetime really lifetime?

Yes. One $149.99 payment, no subscription, includes every future update to Flipped. If the company shuts down (which we don't intend), you keep access for as long as Apple's iOS supervision channel keeps working — the moat is in the OS, not on our servers.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Monthly → Annual upgrade is prorated. Lifetime is a one-way one-time purchase; if you start Monthly or Annual and later want Lifetime, email support and we'll credit your most recent payment toward the upgrade.

Family plan? Team licensing?

Not on the self-serve checkout yet — email support@useflipped.com. Family plans run roughly $5/seat/month and team licenses are negotiated case by case.
