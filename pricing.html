FAQ

What does the 30 flips / 24h cap actually mean?
You can start up to 30 sessions in any rolling 24-hour window. Most users average 1-3 / day; the cap is there to prevent abuse, not real usage.
Will my friend-test access change?
No. Beta users keep the free tier forever, with no cap. You'll never wake up with your account suddenly limited.
