PRICING FAQ

Questions you might have.

Is the 14-day free trial real?

Yes. Monthly and Annual plans both start with a 14-day free trial — no charge until day 15. Cancel any time in the first 14 days and you won't be billed.

What's the cancellation policy?

Monthly: cancel with one click in the customer portal — applies at the end of your billing period.

Annual: prorated refund within 14 days of the renewal (so you can cancel + recover most of your money even after the free trial ends).

Lifetime: 30-day no-questions-asked refund. Email support@useflipped.com.

Why is the Annual plan cheaper per month?

Paying yearly removes the churn-rate guesswork and lowers our payment-processing overhead. The 42% discount passes that saving back. We'd rather have you committed than re-selling you every month.

Is Lifetime really lifetime?

Yes. One $115 payment, no subscription, includes every future update to Flipped. If the company shuts down (which we don't intend), you keep access for as long as Apple's iOS supervision channel keeps working — the moat is in the OS, not on our servers.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Monthly → Annual upgrade is prorated. Lifetime is a one-way one-time purchase; if you start Monthly or Annual and later want Lifetime, email support and we'll credit your most recent payment toward the upgrade.

Family plan? Team licensing?

Not on the self-serve checkout yet — email support@useflipped.com. Family plans run roughly $5/seat/month and team licenses are negotiated case by case.
