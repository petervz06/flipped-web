Why we built it

Every screen-time app breaks the moment you know where to flip the setting back. Flipped doesn't. The restrictions are enforced at the iPhone level — there's no app to delete, no toggle to find, no workaround.

How it works

Plug your iPhone into your Mac, once. Pick which apps to hide. Tap FLIP — distractions disappear from your home screen and Safari blocks their websites. Tap UNFLIP and they come back. That's the whole product.

Will it delete my apps?

No. Flipped hides them while you're flipped. They come back the moment you tap UNFLIP, with all your data intact.

Can I still use Safari?

Yes. Safari stays open for everything except the sites you chose to block — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc. Search, maps, weather, and the rest still work.

What if I want to uninstall?

iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap Flipped → Remove Management. All restrictions come off instantly.
