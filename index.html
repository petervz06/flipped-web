WHAT FLIPPED DOES

▣ Hide the distractions

Pick which apps disappear during a focus session. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, games — gone from the home screen until you unflip.

COMMON QUESTIONS
Will this let me look up porn / adult content?

No. Apple's built-in adult-content filter is active during every session and blocks adult sites automatically. You can't turn it off during a flip.

Can I unflip early in Focus mode?

Focus sessions end automatically at the time you set. One emergency unflip per 3 days is allowed if something urgent comes up. Unplug and Human modes have no early-end option — that's the point.

Does Flipped read my messages or photos?

No. Flipped can hide apps and filter URLs but never reads the contents of any app on your phone. See the privacy policy.

What if I want to stop using Flipped?

Remove it any time: iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → "Remove Management," or use the Mac app's Remove command. All restrictions come off.

Does this work on Android?

Not yet. Android support is on the roadmap.

What if my iPhone breaks / I lose it?

Your Flipped account lives in the cloud. Set up Flipped again on the replacement iPhone with the Mac app — same account, same app blocks, same schedules.
