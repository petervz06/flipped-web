// questions
Will it delete my apps?

No. Flipped hides them while you're flipped. They come back the moment you tap UNFLIP.

Can I still use Safari?

Yes. Safari stays open. Just not Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or whatever else you chose to block.

Can I cheat by deleting the app?

No. Flipped is enforced at the iPhone level, not by an app you can close. That's the point.

What if I want to stop using it?

iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap Flipped → Remove Management. All restrictions come off.
