// common questions
Will this let me look up porn / adult content?

No. Apple's built-in adult-content filter is active during every session and blocks adult sites automatically. You can't turn it off during a flip.

Can I unflip early in Focus mode?

Focus sessions end automatically when the timer hits zero. One emergency unflip per 30 days is allowed if something urgent comes up. Unplug + Forever modes have no early-end option — that's the point.

Does Flipped read my messages or photos?

No. Flipped can hide apps and filter URLs but never reads the contents of any app on your phone.

What if I want to stop using Flipped?

Remove it any time: Mac app → Settings → Remove Flipped, or from your iPhone's Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
