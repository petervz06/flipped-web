WHY FLIPPED EXISTS

"I was Brick and Opal's
best customer."

One Christmas I bought seven Bricks and handed them out to my friends. I believed in this stuff. I'd tried every blocker, every Screen Time limit, every trick.

Then one night — phone locked mid-session, itching to scroll — I opened Settings → Screen Time. And there it was: the off switch. Face ID, one tap, and every "unbreakable" blocker I'd ever paid for just… stopped.

Every tool had an escape hatch. The promise was a lie.

So I built the one that doesn't negotiate. Flipped's key isn't on your phone, and it isn't a password — it's your Mac. And on a Focus or Unplug session there's no workaround at all, not even your Mac. The timer is the only way out.

Everything you need to know
before using Flipped.

01

Works with your Mac.

Flipped pairs your iPhone with your Mac — the Mac is the key that flips and unflips. You'll need one to get set up.

Once you flip, apps come back from your Mac — or on their own when your timer ends. The you of this morning stays in charge, not the you at 1am.
