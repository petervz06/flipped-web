HONESTY

Everything you need to know
before using Flipped.

Flipped is a real commitment. These are the things we'd want you to know before you pay.

01

You need a Mac.

Flipped runs on macOS. The Mac is the only sanctioned way to unflip. Without a paired Mac, you can't use the product.

02

Only your Mac can unflip.

Once flipped, the only way to get blocked apps back is to plug your iPhone into your paired Mac. That's the whole point.

03

Time-based expiry is supported.

Commit for 30 minutes, 1 hour, 8 hours, a week — Flipped will auto-unflip when the timer ends. You don't need access to your Mac to ride out a committed session.

04

Factory reset loses your data, not your flip.

Erasing the iPhone is a nuclear option that wipes all personal data. Supervision persists. Designed this way on purpose.

05

iPhone-only today.

Android and cross-platform coming. iOS is where the moat is sharpest; we built that first.

06

Lost-Mac recovery exists.

If you lose access to your paired Mac, contact support — there's a path to recover, but it isn't instant. Mac stays the key by design.

07

We collect minimal data.

Email, device identifier, app-block history. We do not see your messages, your photos, or your screen content. Read the privacy policy →
