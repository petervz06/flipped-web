Not by an app you can close. There's no toggle to find. No setting to flip back. The restrictions live on your phone, not in software you can quit.

Other apps fail because they ask you to resist. Flipped removes the option entirely. Instagram doesn't appear on your home screen. You can't be tempted by what isn't there.

// built for you, not your kids

This is for adults choosing focus. Not parental controls. Not corporate compliance. Just a tool you point at your own phone when you want your time back.

Will it delete my apps?

No. Flipped hides them while you're flipped. They come back the moment you tap UNFLIP, with all your data intact.

Will my phone still ring?

Yes. Phone, Messages, FaceTime always work. Flipped never blocks anything you'd actually need to live.

What about notifications?

They still come through. You just can't open the app to scroll. Read what arrived, then put the phone down.

Can I cheat by deleting Flipped?

No. The restrictions are enforced at the iPhone level, not by an app. Removing the Mac app doesn't undo them.

How do I uninstall?

iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap Flipped → Remove Management. All restrictions come off instantly.

Does this work on Android?

Not yet. iOS only. The mechanism Flipped uses is iPhone-specific.
