Press kit

For journalists, podcasters, newsletter writers · Last updated 2026-05-26

One-liner

Flipped is a Mac app that hides distracting apps on your iPhone for as long as you want them gone — no jailbreak, no Screen Time gymnastics, no workaround for you to discover later.

Two-paragraph version

Flipped uses the same iOS supervision mechanism Apple ships for schools and enterprises — but applied to your own iPhone, by you. When you tap FLIP on the Mac app, your iPhone hides the apps you've chosen to block (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.) and filters their web equivalents in Safari. When the timer ends, or you tap UNFLIP, your apps reappear instantly. No on-device counter to ignore. No "guilty pleasure" workaround for the determined-enough version of you.

The product is intentionally one-way from your phone: the web flip page lets you start a session from anywhere, but ending one requires the Mac app or the timer expiring. That's the commitment device this category was missing. Built by Pete Albertson, solo, in 2026.

Why it works when others don't

  • Real iOS-level enforcement. Most "screen time" apps run on the iPhone itself — which means they can be disabled on the iPhone itself, by the same person trying to focus. Flipped runs on a Mac which signs and pushes restriction profiles via Apple's MDM channel. The iPhone honors them at the OS level.
  • Granular, customizable, transparent. Pick a preset (Simple / Deep Work / Nomad), or build a custom blocklist app-by-app. See exactly which apps will hide in the active session card.
  • Zero ongoing maintenance. One-time supervision setup (~3 min), then flipping is one tap forever.
  • No data harvesting. Flipped sees app classifications you choose + session timestamps. Never your messages, photos, location, or anything inside any app. Full privacy policy →
