Getting started with Flipped

3 minutes · works on macOS 13+ & iOS 17+

Flipped hides distracting apps on your iPhone for as long as you want them gone — no jailbreak, no Screen Time gymnastics, no workaround for you to discover later. Setup takes about 2-3 minutes the first time, then flipping is one tap.

Before you start

  • You'll need a Mac running macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer.
  • An iPhone running iOS 17 or newer.
  • A USB cable that can sync data (the free cable that came with your iPhone is fine; a charge-only cable from the gas station is not).
  • 5 minutes alone with both devices.

The setup

  1. Go to useflipped.com, drop your email, click SEND ME A KEY. The key arrives within seconds.
  2. Same email — click DOWNLOAD MAC APP, drag Flipped.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Flipped. Paste your license key. The app walks you through 7 short steps.
  4. When it asks: turn off Find My iPhone and Stolen Device Protection (you'll re-enable both at the end). Plug in your iPhone, unlocked.
  5. The app handles the rest. Your iPhone reboots once during setup. When iOS asks "Continue with Partial Setup" or "Erase This iPhone" → tap CONTINUE. Never tap Erase.
  6. One last "Trust" + one last "Enroll This iPhone" tap. You're done.
Important: never tap "Erase This iPhone" during the iOS setup screen. Always tap "Continue with Partial Setup". Erase wipes your data — Continue keeps everything. Flipped's onboarding flags this in two places, but it's worth saying once more.

Your first flip

Out of the box, Flipped's Simple profile blocks Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, X, Facebook, Reddit, Netflix, and a handful of other social/streaming apps — but keeps everything else (Discord, banking, Spotify, Maps, work apps). Tap ▶ FLIP from Home, and your iPhone clears those apps within ~30 seconds. Tap UNFLIP any time to bring them back.

Going stricter

The Modes tab gives you commitment-style flips:

  • Focus (30 min - 24 hr): apps stay hidden until the timer expires. One emergency unflip per month if you really need to bail.
  • Unplug (1 day - 1 year): same, no escape hatch — perfect for vacations or deep deadlines.

The Profiles tab lets you build custom blocklists (or duplicate Simple/Deep Work/Nomad and tweak from there). Custom profiles are local to your Mac.

Tip: organize before you flip

iOS rearranges your home screen each time apps appear/disappear. If that bothers you, put the apps you'll block in a single folder on your iPhone before your first flip. The folder hides as one icon and your home screen layout stays stable.

If something breaks

  • Open Flipped → Settings → Get Help → Copy Diagnostics. Send Pete that text.
  • If the Mac app is stuck: Settings → Reset Everything Local. Wipes only the Mac side; your iPhone supervision stays. Re-paste your license key after to drop right back in.
  • If the iPhone is stuck: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Flipped → Remove Management. Then re-walk the Mac onboarding to re-enroll.

Removing Flipped completely

  • Mac: drag /Applications/Flipped.app to Trash.
  • iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Flipped → Remove Management.
  • Account/data: Mac app → Settings → Danger → Delete Account. Erases everything we have about you, revokes your license keys.
